Case study · Medical equipment · 2023

Wipe-clean case
for VR in the clinic

Hard cases hold VR headsets in foam, and foam can't be disinfected. This project keeps the rugged Pelican case and replaces the foam with one seamless, vacuum-formed tray that a clinician can wipe clean in a single pass.

Developed and tested with clinical users and used by doctors at the University of Kentucky hospital.
